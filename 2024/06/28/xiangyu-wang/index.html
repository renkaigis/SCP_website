June 28, 2024From the SCP archive

President Xiangyu Wang

Fellow, EU Academy of Sciences
Distinguished Professor, East China Jiaotong University

Email: Xiangyu.Wang@curtin.edu.au

Introduction：

Professor Xiangyu Wang, a fellow of the EU Academy of Sciences (EUAS) and recognized as a Highly Cited Researcher (2022, Engineering) by Clarivate Analytics, leads globally in the number of highly cited papers within the fields of Architecture & Building, Civil Engineering, and Transportation in 2022. He was among only ten scientists honored as a Highly Cited Researcher in these domains for the year.

Honors:

  • Clarivate Highly Cited Researcher (Engineering),2022
  • UNESCO Permanent Member of Expert Group, The Bridging Innovation and Learning in TVET(BILT)Project(2019-2025)，UNESCO
  • Fellow, European Union Academy of Sciences,2018
  • Fellow, International Association of Advanced Materials (IAAM).2024
  • Laureate Lecture, IAAM, 2024
  • Albert Nelson Marquis Lifetime Achievement Award,2018
  • Australian Research Council (ARC) College of Experts, 2016
  • Vice President (Industry), International Association on Automation and Robotics in Construction (IAARC), 2024
  • Board of Directors, lAARC, since 2016
  • Board of Directors, International Society of Computing in Civil and Building Engineering (lSCCBE), since 2010
  • Board of Directors, Asian Group for Civil Engineering lnformatics (AGCEl), since 2014
  • Research.com Computer Science in Australia Leader Award, 2022, 2023
  • World’s Top 2% Scientists by Stanford University in 2023, ranking 9,023rd globally, and 58th out of 38,433 in Building and Construction.2023
  • Top 1 ranking in Google Citations in the disciplines of Infrastructure Management, Smart City, and Automation and Robotics. 2024
  • ScholarGPS: named as an inaugural “Highly Ranked Scholar- Lifetime”(Ranks of 0.05% or better out of 30 million scholars) and ranked 13th in the specialty of Augmented Reality worldwide
  • Kyung Hee Award, South Korea, 2018
  • Best Paper Award, International Journal of Production Research,2018
  • Distinction Paper Award, CRIOCM2015
  • Pro-Vice Chancellor Prize for Highest Research Performance, 2013
  • Outstanding performance recognition by Woodside Energy Ltd., 2013
  • Runner-Up Award of Curtin Commercial Innovation Awards, 2012
  • International Scholar Visiting Fellowship, South Korea, 2010.
  • AD Scientific index “World Scientists Rankings”: the 4,581st place globally across all fields “Last 6 years H-index” out of 1,613,800 scientists and the 209th place out of 43,045 scientists across all fields in Oceania World; Overall “H-index’ ranked as the 6th in Civil Engineering in Oceania World out of 514 scientists.
