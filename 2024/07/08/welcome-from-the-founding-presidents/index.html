Welcome from the Founding Presidents

We are delighted to welcome you to the International Society for Smart Construction and Production (SCP, /). As the founding presidents, we are excited to embark on this journey with you towards innovation, collaboration, and excellence in smart construction and production technologies.

The global trend is rapidly moving towards smart construction and production, driving us to seek more efficient and sustainable solutions. Major developed countries are promoting significant initiatives, such as the United States’ “New Building Codes Initiative,” Germany’s “Industrie 4.0,” Japan’s “Society 5.0,” Australia’s “National Reconstruction Fund,”  UK’s “Construction 2025” strategy, and the EU’s “Digital Construction Platform”.

At SCP, we bring together a dynamic community of professionals, researchers, and industry leaders from around the world. Our members include leading academic institutions and industry pioneers. We are honored to have an governance board consisting of Fellows from prestigious academies such as the US National Academy of Engineering, UK Royal Academy of Engineering, European Academy of Sciences and Arts, and Canadian Academy of Engineering.

Joining SCP is an opportunity to connect with a global network of industry leaders, gain access to exclusive resources, and participate in our renowned annual International Conference on Innovative Production and Construction (IPC). By becoming a member, you can contribute to and be recognized for advancements in smart construction and production, celebrating innovation and excellence. We encourage you to join us and be part of this vibrant community driving the future of our industry.

Together, we can achieve our common goals. Through collaboration and innovation, we can tackle industry challenges and drive the continuous development of smart construction and production. Your involvement brings us closer to this goal, and we look forward to working with you to create a brighter future.

Thank you for your interest in the International Society for Smart Construction and Production. Together, we will make significant strides towards a smarter, more sustainable future.

Warm regards,
