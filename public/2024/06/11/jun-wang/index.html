Dr Jun Wang

Western Sydney University, Australia
Email: jun.wang@westernsydney.edu.au

Introduction

Dr. Jun Wang is the Director of the Digital Engineering Lab and an Associate Editor of the International Journal of Construction Management. Renowned for his expertise in the BIM and Digital Engineering field, he has been recognized among the Top 10 BIM Researchers worldwide.  

Honors: 

  • Associate Editor, International Journal of Construction Management
  • Editorial Board Member, MDPI Buildings
  • Editorial Board Member, Intelligent Marine Technology and Systems (IMTS), published by Springer
  • Editorial Review Board Member, Smart and Sustainable Built Environment
  • 2014-2023 Best Paper Award, Frontiers of Engineering Management, 2024,
  • Best Paper Award, The 9th International Conference on Innovative Production and Construction (IPC 2022), 27-30 June 2022 at RMIT University, Melbourne.
  • Best Paper Award, The Australasian Universities Building Education Association (AUBEA) Conference 2019, QLD, Australia.
  • Best Paper Award, International Journal of Production and Research, 2018, Taylor & Francis Group, 2018
  • Excellent paper Award, 23rd International Symposium on Advancement of Construction Management and Real Estate (CRIOCM 2018), Guiyang, China, August 2018
  • Outstanding Reviewer, Automation in Construction, Elsevier, 2017
  • Best Student Presentation award, 3rd International Conference on Civil and Building Engineering Informatics (ICCBEI), 2017
  • Best paper award, 2nd International Conference on Civil and Building Engineering Informatics (ICCBEI), 2015
  • Best Student Member Award, Chartered Institute of Building (CIOB), 2014
  • Best paper award, 31st International Symposium on Automation and Robotics in Construction (ISARC), 2014
  • Best Innovation Concept Award, Curtin University, 2013
  • Second prize, 5th Innovation Competition in Construction and Management, 2012

Fields of research：

  • Building Information Modeling
  • Digital Engineering and Digital Twin
  • Construction Automation and Robotics
  • Generative Planning and Scheduling
  • Digital Asset Management
  • Knowledge Management
