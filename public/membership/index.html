Join the SCP Community

Becoming a member of the International Society for Smart Construction and Production (SCP) connects you with a global network of professionals dedicated to advancing smart construction and production technologies. Our members enjoy exclusive benefits, access to cutting-edge research, and opportunities to collaborate with industry leaders and innovators.

Membership Benefits

As an SCP member, you will receive a range of benefits designed to support your professional growth and enhance your contributions to the field:

  • Access to Exclusive Resources: Gain access to a wealth of resources, including industry reports, research papers, best practice guides, and more.
  • Discounted Conference Fees: Enjoy reduced registration fees for the annual International Conference on Innovative Production and Construction (IPC) and other SCP events.
  • Networking Opportunities: Connect with a global network of professionals, researchers, and industry leaders through our online community, forums, and events.
  • Professional Development: Participate in workshops, webinars, and training sessions to stay updated on the latest advancements and trends in smart construction and production.
  • Recognition and Awards: Be eligible for SCP awards, which recognize outstanding contributions and achievements in the field.
  • Leadership Opportunities: Take on leadership roles within SCP, such as chairing committees, organizing events, or leading local chapters.
  • Publication Opportunities: Submit your research to SCP’s publications and contribute to advancing knowledge in the field.

Types of Membership

SCP offers various membership categories to suit different needs and levels of involvement:

  • Individual Membership: Ideal for professionals and researchers interested in smart construction and production technologies.
  • Institutional Membership: Designed for government organizations and academic institutions that wish to support their staff’s professional development and engage with the global community.
  • Corporate Membership: Provides businesses with the opportunity to showcase their commitment to innovation and gain visibility within the industry.
  • Student Membership: Offers discounted rates and tailored benefits for students pursuing studies in relevant fields, providing them with early exposure to industry trends and networking opportunities.

How to Join

Joining SCP is simple and straightforward. Simply fill out our preliminary membership interest form below, and a representative from our Membership Services team will contact you to discuss your application, answer any questions, and guide you through the next steps. For more information, please reach out to us at secretary@isscpi.com.
