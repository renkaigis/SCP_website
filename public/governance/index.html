International Scientific Committee

The International Scientific Committee is the cornerstone of SCP’s research and innovation activities. Comprising leading researchers and industry experts, the committee guides the development of cutting-edge research, oversees the annual International Conference on Innovative Production and Construction (IPC), and ensures the dissemination of knowledge across the global community.

Youth Professional Committee

The Youth Professional Committee is dedicated to engaging young professionals in the field of smart construction and production. This committee aims to provide a platform for young talent to contribute to SCP’s initiatives, participate in innovative projects, and gain valuable experience.
