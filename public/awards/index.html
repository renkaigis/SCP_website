1. Award Categories and Selection Criteria

The International Society for Smart Construction and Production (SCP) honors excellence and innovation in the field through a variety of award categories:

▷ Lifetime Achievement Award: Honors individuals who have made significant, long-term contributions to the field of smart construction and production.

▪ Selection Criteria: Eligibility for the Lifetime Achievement Award requires nominees to have a minimum of 25 years of research/professional experience in the field of smart construction and production. They must have demonstrated significant, long-term contributions that have advanced the state of smart construction and production. Nominees should have evidence of recognition by peers and industry, such as awards, citations, or other forms of acknowledgment. Proven leadership roles in academia, industry, or government, including mentoring and fostering new talent in the field, are essential. Additionally, nominees must have documented impact on the industry through influential publications, patents, or the implementation of innovative technologies and methodologies.

▷ Young Professional Award: Celebrates emerging leaders under the age of 38 who have demonstrated exceptional promise in the field.

▪ Selection Criteria: To be eligible for the Young Professional Award, nominees must be under the age of 38 as of the award submission deadline. They should have a minimum of 5 years of research/professional experience in smart construction and production. Demonstrated exceptional achievements and contributions to the field, including research, project implementation, or innovative practices, are required. Nominees must show evidence of exceptional promise and potential to become future leaders in the industry.

▷ Excellence in Collaboration Award: Acknowledges successful partnerships between academia, industry, and government that have led to significant advancements in smart construction and production.

▪ Selection Criteria: The Excellence in Collaboration Award is open to partnerships that include at least one representative from academia, and one from industry or government. The partnership must have been active for at least two years. Demonstrated significant advancements in smart construction and production as a direct result of the collaboration are essential. Nominees should provide evidence of innovative approaches or solutions that emerged from the partnership. Additionally, measurable impact on the industry, such as successful projects, publications, policy changes, or technological advancements, is required.

▷ Emerging Technology Award: Celebrates the introduction and integration of new technologies that show great potential in the industry.

▪ Selection Criteria: Eligibility for the Emerging Technology Award requires the technology to be a new introduction or a significant improvement over existing technologies in the field of smart construction and production. The technology must have been implemented in at least one real-world project or pilot program. Demonstrated potential for widespread adoption and significant impact on the industry is essential. Detailed documentation of the technology, including the development process, testing, and results, must be provided.

▷ Community Impact Award: Recognizes projects or initiatives that have had a positive impact on local communities.

▪ Selection Criteria: To be eligible for the Community Impact Award, the project or initiative must be directly related to smart construction and production. Demonstrated engagement and collaboration with local communities are required. Nominees should provide evidence of positive impact on the community, such as improved infrastructure, job creation, environmental benefits, or enhanced quality of life. The project or initiative must have been active for at least one year. Proven sustainability and potential for long-term benefits to the community are also essential.

▷ Smart Construction Award: Celebrates innovative projects that have integrated smart technologies into building and infrastructure development.

▪ Selection Criteria: Eligibility for the Smart Construction Award requires that the project must be completed or significantly advanced within the last three years. The project should demonstrate innovative integration of smart technologies into building or infrastructure development, such as IoT, AI, BIM, digital twins, or advanced sensor networks. Evidence of how these technologies have improved efficiency, safety, sustainability, or overall project outcomes is essential. The project should also showcase successful collaboration among various stakeholders, including developers, contractors, and technology providers.

▷ Smart Production Award: Recognizes advancements in smart production techniques that have improved manufacturing and production processes.

▪ Selection Criteria: To be eligible for the Smart Production Award, the advancements must have been implemented within the last three years and should demonstrate significant improvements in off-site manufacturing and production processes. The innovation should leverage smart production techniques such as automation, AI, IoT, or advanced data analytics to enhance productivity, quality, and efficiency. Evidence of measurable outcomes, such as increased production rates, reduced defects, cost savings, or environmental benefits, must be provided. The nominee should also demonstrate how these advancements can be scaled or adapted across the construction industry.

▷ Digital Transformation Award: Recognizes organizations that have successfully transformed their operations through digital technologies.

▪ Selection Criteria: Eligibility for the Digital Transformation Award requires that the organization must have implemented a comprehensive digital transformation within the last two years. The transformation should encompass significant changes to business operations, processes, and culture through the adoption of digital technologies such as cloud computing, AI, big data, IoT, or blockchain. Evidence of improved operational efficiency, customer satisfaction, innovation capacity, and competitive advantage must be provided. The organization should also demonstrate a clear strategy for ongoing digital innovation and adaptation.

▷ Robotics in Construction Award: Recognizes innovative use of robotics in construction projects to enhance productivity and quality.

▪ Selection Criteria: To be eligible for the Robotics in Construction Award, the use of robotics must have been implemented in construction projects within the last two years. The innovation should demonstrate how robotics have been used to enhance productivity, quality, safety, or efficiency in construction activities. Evidence of successful integration of robotics into the construction workflow and measurable improvements in project outcomes must be provided. The project should also highlight the collaboration between robotics developers, construction firms, and other stakeholders.

3. Nomination Process

SCP encourages the community to nominate deserving candidates for our awards. The nomination process is designed to be transparent and straightforward, ensuring that all deserving contributions are considered. Here’s how you can nominate someone:

Step 1: Identify the Award Category

Review the award categories and determine the most appropriate category for your nominee based on their contributions and achievements.

Step 2: Prepare the Nomination Package

Gather the necessary documents to support your nomination. A complete nomination package typically includes:

  • Nominee’s Resume/CV: Detailed professional background and achievements.
  • Nomination Letter: A letter outlining the nominee’s contributions, impact, and why they deserve the award.
  • Additional Documentation (Optional): Any relevant publications, project summaries, or other supporting materials.

Step 3: Submit the Nomination

Complete the online nomination form available on the SCP website. Attach the nomination package and submit it before the specified deadline. Ensure all required fields are filled and documents are uploaded correctly.

Step 4: Review and Selection

The SCP Awards Committee will review all nominations. The committee, comprising experts from various sectors, evaluates each nomination based on the criteria set for each award category. Finalists may be contacted for additional information or clarification if needed.

Step 5: Announcement and Presentation

Award recipients will be notified by email and publicly announced on the SCP website and at the annual International Conference on Innovative Production and Construction (IPC). Award winners will be invited to receive their awards at the IPC Awards Ceremony.
