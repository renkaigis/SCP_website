The International Society for Smart Construction and Production (SCP) has its origins in the Curtin Advanced Technologies Research and Innovation Alliance (CATRINA), established in 2015. CATRINA was dedicated to pioneering advanced technologies in the construction and production industries. From 2020 to 2024, the society evolved through the Asian Institute of Engineering Brain, further expanding its focus and reach. In 2024, the organization rebranded as SCP, broadening its mission to encompass a global perspective and fostering innovation, collaboration, and knowledge dissemination across academia, industry, and government sectors.

Mission and Vision

Mission: SCP aims to advance the integration of cutting-edge technologies in the construction and production industries. We strive to bridge the gap between academia, industry, and government sectors to create a synergistic environment for developing and applying innovative solutions to real-world challenges.

Vision: To be the leading global organization in promoting smart construction and production technologies, fostering a collaborative network of experts, and driving forward the industry through continuous innovation and knowledge sharing.

Focus Areas: SCP focuses on various areas including smart sensing, construction automation, intelligent production, AI-powered generative solutions, standards and best practices, and training and education.

Organizational Structure

The organizational structure of SCP is designed to support its mission and vision effectively. It includes:

  • Governance Board: Oversees the overall direction and strategy.
  • Vice President: Supports the Governance Board by aligning strategic initiatives across the International Scientific Committee, Youth Professional Committee, Regional Chapters, and Innovation Hubs to foster global research, local engagement, and emerging talent development.
  • International Scientific Committee: Focuses on guiding research and innovation activities.
  • Youth Professional Committee: Provides a platform for young talent to participate in innovative projects, and gain valuable experience
  • Reginal Chapters: Regional branches to facilitate local engagement and activities.
  • Innovation Hubs: Centers for collaborative research and development.

For more detailed information about the committee, please visit our Governance Page.
