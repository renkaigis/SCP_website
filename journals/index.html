Journals

Selected publications advancing research across smart construction and production.

We provide a curated list of esteemed journals that align with our mission to advance cutting-edge research and innovation in the fields of smart construction and production. These journals are highly recommended for their contributions to the body of knowledge and their role in disseminating pioneering research findings. We encourage our members and the broader community to explore, contribute to, and stay updated with the latest advancements through these publications.


Journal of Building Design and Environment

Journal of Building Design and Environment (JBDE) is the Home Journal of the International Society for Smart Construction and Production (SCP). It is a peer-reviewed, open-access journal (Print ISSN 2811-0730; Online ISSN 2811-0749), published triannually by Science Exploration Press. JBDE focuses on ecological architectural design, integrating architectural engineering, architectural aesthetics, sustainable development, and urban planning.

Details

AI in Civil Engineering (AICE)

AI in Civil Engineering (AICE) is the first international and prominent journal to publish original research papers, reviews, comments, and perspectives on AI applications in civil engineering.

Details

Civil Engineering Design

Civil Engineering Design: Holistic Design, construction, maintenance of infrastructures is an international English-language, peer-reviewed, hybrid open access journal published by Ernst & Sohn for research papers as well as practice-oriented reports of all aspects of digitalisation in infrastructure engineering.
